PIN Gate

Added in 5-buildFour digits with no brake was 10,000 guesses on a public URL. Now: six digits, with attempt limiting.
  • The La Condesa wordmark, one six-digit field, one button. On a phone the field opens the numeric keypad.
  • Wrong: "That's not it." in serif under the field.
  • Right: remembered on that device for 30 days, then lands on Count.
Attempt limiting
  • 5 wrong attempts from one IP within 15 minutes lock that IP for 15 minutes.
  • 20 wrong attempts from anywhere within 15 minutes lock the PIN screen for everyone for 15 minutes.
  • While locked, every attempt (even the right PIN) is refused with "Too many tries. Wait 15 minutes." in the same quiet serif line.
  • Every failed attempt is logged with its IP and time, never the digits tried.
Checks
  • A wrong PIN shows "That's not it." and stays on the PIN screen.
  • Six wrong attempts in a row: the sixth is refused with "Too many tries. Wait 15 minutes."
  • The right PIN during a lockout is still refused; after 15 minutes it works again.
  • The attempted PIN never appears in any log.
  • Reopening on the same device within 30 days goes straight to Count.
  • No screen or data request works without a valid PIN session.
Phone · at the cafe · landing screen

Count

Scope said"Pick the cafe, see the last count in grey, update it, save." Now we know: steppers start blank, and only the rows she enters are saved. Nothing is silently rewritten.
  • Cafe picker, preselected to the last cafe she counted. One cafe at a time.
  • Rows: product name, stepper (− number +), and in grey under the name "last counted Oct 9: 3".
  • Save is pinned to the bottom. After saving, the grey lines update to today, the steppers clear, and "Saved" shows for 2 seconds.
  • Past counts (added in 5-build; she keeps a paper book of old counts today): one link under the cafe picker opens a plain read-only list for the selected cafe, newest day first, each day's date with the product and number for each row saved that day. No editing, no charts. A cafe never counted shows "No counts yet."
States
  • All rows blank: Save is disabled.
  • Unsaved entries and she switches cafe: a one-time prompt.
  • Unsaved entries and she leaves via the nav: the same prompt
  • Never counted: "not counted yet"
Checks
  • Corazon Cocina shows its standing products with blank steppers and grey last counts.
  • Entering two numbers and saving updates only those two grey lines; the other rows keep their old dates.
  • Save is disabled when every row is blank.
  • Switching cafes with unsaved entries prompts once.
  • Saving a count changes no invoice, and creating an invoice changes no count.
  • After two saves on different days, Past counts shows both dates, newest first, with the right numbers under each.

Deliver

Scope said"Sequential invoice numbers." Now we know it's Eileen's own scheme: location code + MMDDYYYY, stored as text, suggested, overwritable and unique.
  • Cafe picker · Date (the invoice date and delivery date, default today) · Invoice # (pre-filled suggestion).
  • Each standing product: name, price, a blank qty field, a live line total, and the grey shelf count (reference only, never pre-fills).
  • A running total in integer cents. Create invoice is disabled until some qty is above 0.

  • The suggestion follows the date and cafe until she types her own number; then it's left alone.
  • A duplicate shows "Already used on Oct 3 for Corazon SB" and won't save.
  • A same-day repeat gets a suffix she types herself (110172026-2). The app never invents one.
Prices, special orders & snapshots
  • The unit price on each line is editable, pre-filled from the product or the saved copy. It changes only that invoice.
  • "+ Add special order" adds a line with a description, price and qty. It's not linked to a product, prints like any line, and never joins the standing list or Count. The description is required, the price is 0 or more, and the qty is at least 1.
  • Every invoice saves a copy of its lines and the cafe details. Past invoices never change when prices change.
Edit
  • The same screen, loaded with an existing invoice: quantities, add or remove lines, date, number. Totals recompute.
  • Changing the cafe is in only if it comes free; otherwise she voids and recreates.
Checks
  • Corazon Cocina loads its standing list with prices and grey counts; every qty is blank.
  • Line and invoice totals update immediately and are correct to the cent.
  • Corazon SB on Oct 17, 2026 suggests 110172026, which changes with the date or cafe until she types over it.
  • A duplicate number blocks the save with "Already used on [date] for [cafe]".
  • Create invoice is disabled with no quantities.
  • Changing a line's unit price changes that invoice only; the product's price is unchanged on the next new delivery.
  • A special order line with a description, price and qty prints on the invoice; a missing description, negative price or qty of 0 blocks the save.
  • Editing one quantity updates the total on the Invoice screen and in the Ledger.

Invoice

Scope said"Matched to her Canva form." Now we know: only the delivered lines print, the Corazon heart logo prints when the cafe has one, and nothing is ever deleted (void instead).
  • The Canva form: wordmark, INVOICE, "Invoice for:" block with the logo, Invoice # and Date (printed as 10/17/2026, learner-accepted in 5-build), DESCRIPTION / QTY / PRICE / TOTAL, a dotted rule, INVOICE TOTAL $, the blush contact block, "Order received by:", and the Net 30 line.
  • Type: INVOICE in Limelight · body text in a Times-first serif stack, with Tinos next (added in 5-build: metric-compatible with Times New Roman, so the PDF, rendered on a server with no system fonts, breaks lines exactly where her printout does) · the wordmark, footer title and column headers are Canva PNGs (DK Otago's license forbids web use, so the font never enters the repo; the images go in design/ before Block 3).
  • A grey status line: "Last sent Oct 17, 8:12pm" · "Last sent Oct 17, 8:12pm · last try failed" after a failed later attempt · "Not sent" if no send ever succeeded · or nothing if never emailed.
  • Navigation (phone first, added in 5-build): the Invoice screen keeps the bottom nav and has a "← Back to Ledger" link at the top. Every sheet (Email pop-up, confirms, Mark paid) closes with Cancel, a tap outside it, or the phone's back button and returns her to the same screen; Back never jumps to the previous page while a sheet is open.
  • Actions: Print (browser dialog, one Letter page, no app chrome; cancelling leaves her on the Invoice screen, state intact) · Download PDF (the same file the email attaches, between Print and Send email; served as an attachment, so it downloads and the app tab never navigates to the PDF) · Send email (opens the Email pop-up; reads Send again after the first successful send) · Edit · Void (one confirm, "Void" stamp, number retired).
Checks
  • The printout laid next to the Canva reference matches its layout, fonts, header, footer and Net 30 line, with only the delivered lines.
  • Corazon invoices show the heart; Little Heart and Alma leave that space empty.
  • It fits one Letter page with no nav or buttons.
  • Download PDF saves Invoice-[number].pdf, identical to the email attachment.
  • At 390px, after Download PDF the Invoice screen is still showing with its buttons.
  • Cancel and the phone's back button both close the Email pop-up and leave her on the invoice.
  • Void asks once, stamps "Void", and the number stays taken.
Pop-up over the Invoice screen

Email Invoice

The Email pop-up
  • To: the invoice's saved billing email, filled in when the pop-up opens (no waiting on a second request), editable for this send only. She never types the recipient; her only required action is one tap on Send email.
  • Subject: "La Condesa Baking Company Invoice 110172026", fixed
  • Body: the fixed template with cafe, total and due date, collapsed to its first two lines with Show full email
  • "Invoice-110172026.pdf attached"
  • Cancel · Send email (black, one tap), pinned to the bottom so they're always visible without scrolling, on phone and laptop. After a send: Send again, with a grey "Last sent Oct 17, 8:12pm" line above it.
Outcomes
  • Success: the pop-up closes, "Last sent [date, time]" shows on the invoice in grey, the button reads Send again, and the Ledger row shows the envelope with "Emailed [date]".
  • Failure: "Couldn't send. The invoice is saved. Try again or print it." plus a Try again button. The invoice shows "Not sent", and she can send it again later. Never a false "Sent".
  • No email saved: the To field is empty with the cursor in it, "No email saved for this invoice. Type one to send.", and Send email stays disabled until there's an address.

From invoices@lacondesabakingcompany.com · Reply-To her Gmail. Until the domain verifies, sends go from Resend's onboarding sender, which only delivers to the account owner's inbox; the sender and Reply-To live in env, so the switch is two values and a redeploy (5-build, slice 8) · the invoice saves before sending · Send again keeps the same number and subject.

Checks
  • On camera, a real email with the PDF arrives in the learner's inbox, and the logged message ID matches it.
  • No email saved blocks Send email until one is typed.
  • A forced failure shows the failure line and "Not sent", never a "Last sent" line.
  • On a 390×640 phone and a laptop, Cancel and Send email are visible without scrolling, even with the full email shown.
  • An invoice with no saved email opens with the cursor in To, "No email saved for this invoice. Type one to send.", and Send email disabled.
  • No button or label anywhere says "Resend". After a send, the Invoice page, the pop-up and the Ledger say "Last sent …", "Send again" and "Emailed …".

Every screen

Added in 5-buildA "?" on every page, words beside every icon, and a send button that never says "Resend" (also the email provider's name).
How it works
  • A small "?" in the top corner of every page, the PIN screen included, opens a plain-words page.
  • One short section each for Count, Deliver, Invoice page, Email pop-up and Ledger: what the screen is for and what each button does, using the buttons' exact names.
Words, not just icons
  • No icon without words: every icon comes with words she can read, such as the envelope with "Emailed Oct 17". On a laptop, hovering shows the full detail, and screen readers hear the same words.
  • Button names: the send button reads Send email the first time and Send again after a successful send, on the Invoice page, in the Email pop-up, in the Ledger and on the How it works page.

Operational requirements

Logging, from block 1
  • One JSON line per server event: time, route, outcome, duration, plus the message ID or error for sends.
  • Never logs emails, addresses, phones, invoice bodies, the PIN or env vars. Only the invoice number and cafe name.
  • The host's function logs are the viewer.
Health and client
  • /health: database status and app version (cold start vs outage).
  • Every request failure shows a one-line message, never a blank screen, and logs to the console with the route name.

Boundary

  • The email template editor: one code file for now.
  • The full printed/emailed history line, and the "(revised)" / "Edited after sending" wording: a second send is just Send again for now.

  • Real data, the production PIN, and Eileen's acceptance run.
Later
  • Stripe · multi-user · low-stock alerts · a bake list · logo upload
  • Overdue reminders (stub, added in 5-build; not built now): a daily Vercel Cron job finds unpaid, non-void, past-due invoices. Ask-first by default: Eileen gets one email listing them, with a single signed link that sends the reminders in one tap. The reminder reuses the invoice PDF with a short Net 30 body, and the Ledger row shows "Reminded [date]". Fully automatic per cafe is a later toggle.
  • Export the Ledger as Excel (.xlsx) (stub, added in 5-build; not built now): respects the cafe filter and a date range. Columns: invoice number, cafe, delivery date, due date, total, status, paid date, reference.
  • Accounts receivable aging report (stub, added in 5-build; not built now): unpaid invoices grouped by how overdue (current, 1 to 30, 31 to 60, 61 to 90, over 90 days), per cafe and in total. Exportable as PDF or Excel, for Eileen's accountant or for following up on overdue bills.
Never
  • Ingredients or recipe costing · accounting or tax · model-generated prices, totals, dates, numbers or email text · inventory decrement · real data in the repo · hard delete · app-invented number suffixes

Build rules for spec & build

One file, one responsibility, and no god files. Every code file starts with a one-sentence Job: comment; if it needs a second sentence, the file gets split. Flag any code file over about 200 lines at each checkpoint.
